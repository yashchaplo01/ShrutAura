What Our Fractional CFOs Deliver

Forward-looking financial intelligence designed to protect cash, maximize margins, and unlock valuation.

13-Week Rolling Cash Forecasting

A dynamic, direct-method cash flow model tracking weekly cash collections, vendor disbursements, payroll peaks, and net burn to avoid cash crunches.

  • Direct cash flow model updated bi-weekly
  • Working capital & runway sensitivity toggles
  • Variance analysis vs. prior week actuals

SaaS & Unit Economics Metrics

Deep-dive financial telemetry for recurring revenue models. We calculate CAC, LTV, Magic Number, gross revenue retention (GRR), and net retention (NRR).

  • Cohort-based LTV to CAC payback curves
  • Burn multiple & Rule of 40 scoring
  • MRR/ARR waterfall (New, Expansion, Churn)

Board & Investor Relations Packs

Institutional-grade presentation materials for quarterly board meetings, shareholder letters, and investor updates that highlight performance and strategic priorities.

  • Polished board slide decks in PowerPoint/Google Slides
  • Executive variance commentary & qualitative analysis
  • Preparation 7 days prior to board meetings

Scenario & Headcount Modeling

Multi-scenario models for aggressive growth, base case, and downside scenarios. Evaluate the true fully-loaded cost and cash impact of new hires and marketing spend.

  • Interactive dynamic driver toggles
  • Hiring plan ramp & commission tiers
  • Sensitivity matrix on pricing and churn

Fundraising & M&A Due Diligence

Be ready when institutional investors or acquirers knock. We build the virtual data room (VDR), prepare 3-statement models, and address diligence Q&A.

  • Audit-proof virtual data room construction
  • Quality of Earnings (QofE) defense
  • Cap table and dilution modeling

Pricing & Margin Optimization

Analyze customer and product gross margins to identify unprofitable contracts, optimize tier structures, and accelerate free cash flow generation.

  • Customer concentration & margin analysis
  • Value-based pricing model simulations
  • Working capital release recommendations
