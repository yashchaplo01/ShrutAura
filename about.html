Meet the Founder

I started ShrutAura Global to give accounting and CPA firms a back-office partner they can rely on, one that combines sound technical judgement with efficient, modern processes.

My foundation is built on years of hands-on work. I completed a full three-year articleship, where I worked on accounting, audit, taxation, and compliance across a wide range of businesses. I then worked in a Big 4 environment at EY, reviewing IT general controls for a leading listed company, where I also built an automation tool to cut manual data extraction from SAP and a Power BI dashboard for resource planning.

Today, I bring that same discipline to every client engagement: accurate books, timely month-end closes, and clear communication.
